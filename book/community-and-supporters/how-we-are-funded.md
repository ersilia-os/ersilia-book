# How we are funded

Ersilia is a nonprofit organization, and our work is possible thanks to the support of foundations, institutions, companies and individual donors. This support comes in different forms:

* **Grants and funding** from foundations and public institutions that support our projects and core activities.
* **Donations** from individuals and organizations who believe in our mission.
* **In-kind support** from companies and organizations that provide services, software, computing resources, or skills-based volunteering and mentoring.

We are grateful to all our supporters. You can see the full list of funders and in-kind supporters on the [About page](https://ersilia.io/about/) of our website.
