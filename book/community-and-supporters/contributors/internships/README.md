# Internships

Internships give early-career scientists and developers hands-on experience in open-source research software, and they bring new ideas and skills to our team. We pay particular attention to giving opportunities to women in STEM, who remain underrepresented in both technology and the life sciences. This is also part of our [Gender Equality Plan](https://ersilia.gitbook.io/ersilia-book/about-us/values-and-commitment/gender-equality-plan).

Since 2022, more than 35 interns have worked with Ersilia. Most of them joined through [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/), a program that offers paid remote internships in open source to people from groups underrepresented in tech. Applicants start by installing the Ersilia Model Hub, testing some of our models and training their own model on a public dataset. The selected interns then join the team for three months, mostly working on the Model Hub: adding new models, fixing and testing existing ones, and improving our code and documentation. Some of them have stayed with us afterwards as maintainers or mentors.

We also host interns from universities and research centers, such as [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/), [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/) and [IRB Barcelona](https://www.irbbarcelona.org/), as well as through programs like the [Laidlaw Foundation](https://laidlawscholars.network/) and [Sage Corps](https://sagecorps.org/). These internships are usually linked to a specific project, from research to software development or communications.

You can see all our interns in the [Community and Alumni](https://ersilia.io/about/) section of our website.
