# Volunteers

Volunteers are an essential part of the Ersilia community. Since 2022, more than 50 volunteers have contributed their time and expertise to our work. Many have joined through employee volunteering and university programs, including teams from [GitHub](https://github.com/), [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/), the [Atlassian Foundation](https://www.atlassianfoundation.org/) and the [Good Data Institute](https://www.gooddatainstitute.com/), and students from [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu/) and the [University of California, Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/). Others have joined independently. Volunteers and mentors support Ersilia in areas such as software development, data science, mentoring and strategic advice, and help us extend our capacity beyond what our core team could achieve alone.

You can see all our volunteers in the [Community and Alumni](https://ersilia.io/about/) section of our website.
