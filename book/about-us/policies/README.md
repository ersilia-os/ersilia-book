# Policies

This section brings together the policies that govern how we develop our tools and handle information. It includes our **Open standards and best practices**, which describe the principles we follow to keep our work open, reproducible and accessible. Our **AI Policy** sets out how we use artificial intelligence responsibly across all our activities. Finally, our **Privacy notice** explains how we collect, use and protect personal data.
