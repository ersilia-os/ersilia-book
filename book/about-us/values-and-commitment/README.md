# Values & Commitment

This section sets out the principles that guide how we work as a community. It includes our **Diversity and inclusion statement**, which describes our commitment to an open and inclusive environment. It also includes our **Gender Equality Plan**, which defines concrete actions to promote gender equality within the organization. Finally, our **Code of conduct** sets the standards of behavior expected from everyone who takes part in Ersilia, including staff, contributors, interns and volunteers.
